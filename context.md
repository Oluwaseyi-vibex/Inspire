# Project Context: Inspire Nigeria Child Project

## Overview

**Inspire Nigeria Child Project** is a web platform dedicated to empowering children in the Niger Delta region through quality education and impactful learning experiences. The project aims to remove barriers to education, ensuring that no child is left behind by leveraging thoughtful design and innovative solutions.

## Mission

Establish a platform for children to challenge societal norms that perpetuate negative influences. Empower kids to serve as ambassadors and lifestyle models, inspiring and educating their peers.

## Vision

The inspire Nigeria Child Project emerges as a beacon of hope. This initiative is a consciously designed platform aimed at reorienting the Nigerian child to counteract the negative influences they encounter daily. The project addresses the critical developmental stages of adolescence, a period where moral standards and life awareness are shaped.

## Background

The Niger Delta schools conference, themed "Values reorientation: the hope for a better Nigeria," brings together students from across the region to discuss key issues facing their communities and the nation. Students engage in essay writing and speech presentations on important sub-topics including:

- **Oil theft** and the impact of oil subsidy removal on the Nigerian economy
- **Oil spillage** effects on the Niger Delta environment and biodiversity
- **My Niger Delta Dream** — envisioning a thriving, sustainable, and prosperous future
- **Tech and digital skills development** for economic growth and innovation

Winners in each category have the opportunity to represent their states at the conference in Yenagoa City, Bayelsa State.
