### **An Insight • inspire NIGERIACHILD**

The Nigerian society we experience today is far removed from the aspirations and dreams of its founding visionaries. Particularly in the Niger Delta, the plight of the Nigerian child and their environment paints a grim picture of what the future may hold if urgent action is not taken. These children, often referred to as the leaders of tomorrow, face challenges that threaten to rob them of their cultural identity, moral foundation, and opportunities for self-actualization.

Our children today are scarcely taught the meaning and significance of our national anthem and pledge, let alone inspired to defend them. The encroaching influence of Western pop culture is rapidly eroding our cultural heritage and values, leaving many children unable to speak or understand their native languages.

---

### **The Vision Behind InspireNigeriaChild**

The InspireNigeriaChild Project emerges as a beacon of hope. This initiative is a consciously designed platform aimed at reorienting the Nigerian child to counteract the negative influences they encounter daily. The project addresses the critical developmental stage of adolescence, a period where moral standards and life awareness are shaped.

At this stage, young people are most vulnerable, often making choices that could lead them down paths of regret and destruction. These choices, compounded by peer pressure, have long-term consequences that may not be immediately visible but significantly impact their futures.

The InspireNigeriaChild Project recognizes these challenges and seeks to create a supportive environment to guide children and adolescents toward positive outcomes.

### **OBJECTIVES**

1. **Challenging the Status Quo**
   Establish a platform for children to challenge societal norms that perpetuate negative influences. Empower kids to serve as ambassadors and lifestyle models, inspiring and educating their peers.

2. **Reorientation Against Negative Influences**
   Conduct targeted reorientation exercises to address harmful societal influences. Utilize strategically developed programs to sensitize and enlighten children on avoiding pitfalls, fostering the need for change, and cultivating a better understanding of life choices.

3. **Promoting Family Awareness**
   Emphasize the importance of healthy parent-child relationships. Address critical health concerns, including HIV/AIDS awareness and other life-threatening health issues.

4. **Advocating for Civic Responsibility and Child Rights**
   Educate children about the Child Rights Act and their civic responsibilities. Promote the importance of upholding values that contribute positively to society.

5. **Discovering and Projecting Talents**
   Identify children with exceptional talents and skills. Provide platforms to enhance and showcase these talents within Nigeria's fast-growing markets.

6. **Reviving Cultural Heritage**
   Work towards reviving and promoting Nigerian cultural heritage and values among children. Counter the erosion of native languages and traditions by integrating cultural pride into educational activities.

7. **Building Strategic Partnerships**
   Forge a uniquely effective collaboration involving government bodies, private sector stakeholders, and the inspireNigeriaChild organization. Leverage this partnership to encourage young people to embark on paths of self-discovery and excellence.

### **APPROACH AND ACTIVITIES**

The InspireNigeriaChild Project deploys a variety of inspiring and entertaining activities to engage its target audience. By creating an atmosphere of learning and interaction, the project seeks to achieve its objectives through programs that are:

- **Interactive:** Encouraging participation and dialogue among children.

- **Culturally Inclusive:** Incorporating elements of Nigerian heritage into activities.

- **Awareness-Driven:** Addressing pressing social, health, and developmental issues.

- **Skill-Oriented:** Providing avenues for talent discovery and skill enhancement.

### **ABOUT THE CONFERENCE**

The Niger Delta schools conference, themed "Values reorientation: the hope for a better Nigeria," is set to bring together students from across the region to discuss key issues facing their communities and the nation as a whole. As part of the conference, students will be writing essays and giving speech presentations on a number of important sub-topics. These presentations will not only allow students to showcase their knowledge and research skills, but also to engage in critical thinking about the pressing issues facing their communities.

One of the key sub-topics that students will be focusing on is oil theft and the impact of oil subsidy removal on the Nigerian economy. This issue is of critical importance to the Niger Delta region, which is home to a significant portion of Nigeria's oil reserves. By studying and discussing this issue, students will be able to better understand the complexities of the country's oil economy and the challenges that come with it. They will also have the opportunity to propose potential solutions to address these issues and promote sustainable development in the region.

Another important sub-topic that students will be addressing is the effects of oil spillage on the Niger Delta environment. The Niger Delta region has long been plagued by environmental degradation due to oil exploration activities, leading to widespread pollution and loss of biodiversity. By examining this issue and its impact on their communities, students will be able to raise awareness about the environmental challenges facing the region and advocate for greater environmental protection measures.

In addition to these pressing issues, students will also be encouraged to dream about the future of the Niger Delta region and what it could become. The sub-topic "My Niger Delta Dream" will give students the opportunity to envision a future where their communities are thriving, sustainable,
and prosperous. By sharing their dreams and aspirations for the region, students will be able to inspire others and work towards creating a better future for themselves and future generations.

Lastly, students will also be focusing on tech and digital skills development, recognizing the importance of technology in driving economic growth and innovation in the 21st century. By developing their skills in this area, students will be better equipped to contribute to the development of their communities and the country as a whole.

Overall, the Niger Delta schools conference presents a unique opportunity for students to engage in meaningful discussions about the challenges and opportunities facing their communities. By participating in essay writing and speech presentations on these important sub-topics, students will be able to raise awareness, inspire change, and work towards building a better future for themselves and their communities. The winners in each category will have the opportunity to represent their states at the conference grand finale in the host City, showcasing their knowledge and commitment to positive change.

## **Executive Summary: 19th Grand Converge**

**Theme:** "Values Reorientation - The Hope for a Better Nigeria"

The 19th annual Niger Delta Schools Conference, themed Values Reorientation - The Hope for a Better Nigeria, is set to be a transformative platform for empowering the youth and reshaping societal values. This year's Grand Converge will focus on seven critical areas that address the holistic development of students and prepare them to contribute meaningfully to national growth:

1. **Career Paths:** Guiding participants in understanding and navigating their professional aspirations to unlock their full potential.

2. **Drug Use and Abuse Sensitization:** Educating attendees on the dangers of substance abuse and the importance of making informed life choices.

3. **Mental Health Awareness:** Promoting mental well-being through discussions on emotional resilience and support systems.

4. **Gender Equality:** Encouraging inclusivity and equity by fostering awareness of gender rights and opportunities.

5. **Identifying Talents and Skills:** Helping students discover and develop their innate abilities for personal and professional fulfilment.

6. **Tech and Digital Skills Development:** Equipping participants with 21st-century digital competencies for a technology-driven world.

7. **My Culture, My Pride, My Identity:** Celebrating cultural heritage while instilling a sense of pride and identity in participants.

The project organizing committee, after extensive consultations, has assembled a distinguished lineup of panelists and speakers—experts and thought leaders passionate about these focal areas. With their commitment and insight, the conference promises to be an impactful and inspiring event, fostering a generation of responsible, skilled, and value-driven individuals who are the hope for a better Nigeria.

## **THE IDEA**

The 19th Edition of Inspired Niger Delta Schools Conference is strategically designed to tour through the nine Niger Delta states, holding states Conferences and Preliminaries that is planned to host over 100 secondary schools represented with a minimum of 30 students from each school and their parents/Guardians respectively in the state level.

The Preliminaries will feature selected students in the shortlisted categories for essay writing and speech presentations on the selected topics. (Two students will represent each on the shortlisted categories, one to write an essay and the other to make a speech presentation.)

The Preliminaries will hold at least 5days to the states Conference. The best 4 speech presentations will be featured on the stages of the states conference while the best 4 essay article will be published on the Inspire Magazine.

Bayelsa and Delta State(s) will play host to the grand converge "Niger Delta Schools Conference". A high-powered student delegation from (the schools that produced the winners in the preliminary categories across the nine Niger Delta states, representing their states respectively via the schools).

This grand converge is developed to be a 3days inspiring gathering of young minds who are the future of the Niger Delta region and the nation at large. Here all the finalists from the state level will have their final round battle to select the top 4 in each category. The conference is designed in phases that is very educative and very inspiring in every segment. These 3 days will feature activities like: Creative Art and Craft Exhibition, Panel Discussions, Lectures from guest speakers, Concert and Awards, Excursion and History documentation, games amongst others.

This project is aimed at gathering concerned stakeholders on a platform to discuss the advancement of the Niger Delta child.
